## The Honest Picture

A 55 km MTB race in KZN bushveld terrain is a **3.5–4.5 hr endurance event** for a first-timer. It's roughly **2.3× your current longest MTB ride** and includes race-pace effort, terrain demands, and nutrition demands you haven't tested yet.

The plan is conservative on purpose:
- Build long-ride volume systematically (no jumping >25% week-to-week)
- Dress-rehearse 2 weeks out (50 km at race intensity, race kit, race nutrition)
- Mini-taper, race, recover

**The goal is to finish strong and intact** — not to race for time. Time goals come at year 2.

## Nutrition Rehearsal

**Test on W11 (6 Jun) and W12 (13 Jun) rides.**

### During the ride

| Time mark | Fuel |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1 bottle on bike (sip from start) |
| 0:30 | First gel/energy bar (~30 g carbs) |
| 1:00 | Second bottle (refill if available) + half banana or bar |
| 1:30 | Gel (~30 g carbs) |
| 2:00 | Real food — bar, half sandwich, dried fruit |
| 2:30 | Gel |
| 3:00 | Bar |
| 3:30+ | Gel + water only — body is full at this point |

**Target: 60–80 g carbs/hour** for race intensity. That's ~2 gels OR 1 gel + 1 bar per hour, with sports drink mixed in.

### Hydration
- 1 bottle (750 ml) per hour minimum
- Sports drink in bottle 1, water in bottle 2
- Electrolyte tabs if you're a salty sweater
- Pee before the start. You should pee mid-race if you're hydrating right.

### What to test specifically on the 13 Jun ride
- [ ] Which gels sit OK in your stomach (test brands BEFORE the rehearsal — too late on race day)
- [ ] Can you eat a bar while riding without stopping?
- [ ] Bottle refill plan if no support — carbohydrate powder pre-loaded in spare bottle?
- [ ] Caffeine: test on 13 Jun ride if you plan to use on race day. Don't introduce on race day.

## Pacing Strategy

> [!warning]
> **The #1 first-timer mistake: going too hard in the first hour.** Race-day adrenaline + fresh legs + group pace → you feel great → you blow up at km 30–40. Resist.

### Effort plan

| Phase | Km | Effort | HR target | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Settle** | 0–15 (Hr 0–1) | RPE 5–6 | ≤155 | Conversational. Let groups go. |
| **Steady** | 15–35 (Hr 1–2) | RPE 6–7 | 150–165 | Working but sustainable. Eat + drink. |
| **Race** | 35–50 (Hr 2–3) | RPE 7 | 160–170 | This is where you start picking off others. |
| **Empty tank** | 50–55 (Hr 3+) | RPE 8 | 165–180 | Last 5 km — give what you have left. |

### Discipline rules
1. **First hour HR must stay ≤155.** Set Garmin alarm.
2. **Eat by the clock, not by feel.** Every 30 min from 0:30. By the time you feel hungry, you're already bonking.
3. **Drink before you're thirsty.** Sip every 10 min.
4. **Technical sections — survive, don't race.** A crash at km 20 ends your day. The race is won on the climbs and the flat power, not the technical descents.
5. **Don't race the next person.** Race the clock and the fuel plan.

### Friday before race
- Check tyre pressure, sealant, chain lube
- Lay out full kit
- Set 3 alarms
- Carb-focused dinner — pasta, rice, potatoes — nothing new or spicy
- 600 ml water in evening
- Asleep by 21:30

### Race morning
- Wake 3 hr before start
- Breakfast 2.5–3 hr before: oats with honey + banana + coffee (~80–100 g carbs)
- 500 ml water over the next 90 min
- Arrive 60 min early
- 20 min warmup spin + 2 × 1 min build to race pace
- Final pee
- 1 gel 15 min before start
- Get to start chute 10 min before

## Stop Signs (Mid-Race)

These mean stop and reassess (don't push through):

1. **Sharp pain anywhere** — not just discomfort. Specific stabbing pain in a joint or muscle.
2. **Heel pain >3/10** — PF flare. Don't make it worse.
3. **Inability to keep food/water down** for >30 min — hydration crisis brewing.
4. **HR drifting up at same power** for sustained 20+ min — overheating or dehydration.
5. **Mechanical you can't fix in 10 min** — DNF is better than walking 30 km out.

There's no shame in pulling out. Imfolozi will be there next year. Your foot, knee, and back have to last to Sept/Oct (sub-60 attempt) and beyond.
